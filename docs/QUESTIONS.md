# Sample Questions for the AI Data Platform

Questions you can ask the system based on the current metrics and semantic models.

---

## Revenue

- What is the total revenue?
- What is the total revenue this month?
- What is the total revenue by country?
- Which country generates the most revenue?
- Which city generates the most revenue?
- What is the revenue trend over the last 30 days?
- What is the revenue by order status?
- What is the revenue in France?
- Compare revenue between the United States and Japan.
- What is the revenue in Paris vs Berlin?

---

## Orders

- How many orders were placed in total?
- How many orders were placed this week?
- How many orders are pending?
- How many orders were completed last month?
- Which country has the most orders?
- Which city has the most orders?
- What is the daily order volume over the last 7 days?
- What is the order count by status?
- How many orders were placed in France this month?
- Which city has the most completed orders?
- What is the order breakdown by country?

---

## Branch Performance

- Which branch country has the highest sales?
- Which branch city has the most orders?
- What is the total revenue per branch country?
- What is the total revenue per branch city?
- How does Paris compare to New York in total revenue?
- Which country has the best order completion rate?

---

## Customers

- How many customers do we have?
- How many new customers registered this month?
- Which country has the most customers?
- Which city has the most customers?
- How many customers were acquired over the last 90 days?

---

## Campaigns

- How many campaigns do we have in total?
- How many campaigns are active right now?
- What is the total ad spend across all platforms?
- What is the total revenue attributed to campaigns?
- What is the total budget allocated across all campaigns?
- Which platform has the highest total spend?
- Which platform generates the most revenue?

### Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- What is the overall CTR across all campaigns?
- Which platform has the best CTR?
- What is the CTR for Meta campaigns this month?
- Compare the CTR between TikTok and Google.

### Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- What is the overall ROAS?
- Which platform has the best ROAS?
- What is the ROAS for Meta campaigns?
- Which campaign has the highest ROAS?

### Cost Per Click (CPC)
- What is the average CPC across all platforms?
- Which platform has the lowest CPC?
- What is the CPC for Google campaigns this month?

### Impressions & Conversions
- How many total impressions were generated?
- How many total conversions were recorded?
- Which platform has the most impressions?
- Which platform has the most conversions?
- What is the total number of clicks across all campaigns?

---

## Products & Order Items

- How many units were sold in total?
- Which product has the most units sold?
- Which product generates the most revenue?
- What is the average unit price across all products?
- What is the total revenue from order line items?
- Which product sells the most in France?
- Which product sells the most in Japan?
- What is the revenue per product this month?
- Which city buys the most units?
- Which country has the highest product revenue?
- What is the best selling product in the United States?
- How many units of each product were sold last month?

### Campaign × Product Performance
- Which campaign's product generated the most orders during the campaign period?
- What is the actual revenue vs reported revenue for Meta campaigns?
- Which platform has the best ROAS when compared to actual observed sales?
- Which product promoted by TikTok sold the most units?
- What is the revenue delta between campaign-reported and observed sales for Google?
